## Stage C4b: a fill can last half a beat (1/2 BEAT)

ROADMAP "Samples in one place" (C4b), FILL: SAMPLE ("LENGTH 1/2 BEAT ... 2 BARS"); the half beat was left out of part 7 and promised.

- **1/2 BEAT** is a fill length next to 1, 2, 4 and 8 beats, for every fill kind (a quick sample hit or a short break just before the next brick). It is chosen in the same places: LENGTH on the FILL page and in the scene / brick fill windows, Length in the right-click menu, the left edge of the yellow block on TRACK (it snaps to 1/2 too).
- Half a beat follows the grid: 2 steps on 1/16, 4 on 1/32, 1 on the triplet grid (rounded down, never 0). A SAMPLE fill on STRETCH is fitted to that window.
- Stored as fill beats 0 (`kFillHalfBeat`, `FillStepsOf`, `FillBeatsOf`, `FillLenLabel` in `src/core/ArrangeTypes.h`). **Project format 10**: an older TR-Brick would read 0 as one beat, so it now keeps such a file and saves a copy (DATA-1) instead of writing over it.
