## Tests: the UI test presses every labelled button once

- `t8_uitest` gained a SWEEP between the tour and the random clicks. In 11 screen setups (LIVE, LIVE on a synth, each tool, TRACK NOTES / SOUND / MIX on a drum and a synth, CHORD LAB on TRACK), it finds every control with a label and presses each one once. Before each press the setup is made again; after it, Esc twice closes what opened. That is about 1,200 presses per run.
- The test finds the controls through ImGui's test-engine hooks (on only in this test's own ImGui copy).
- Why: in the review of the UI test, the random clicks found the missing scene locks of the SOUND view only after about 60 seeds. The sweep finds them on every run: with those locks taken out again, it fails at "THIS BRICK ONLY".
- The test now also fails if fewer than 100 controls were pressed in the sweep.
