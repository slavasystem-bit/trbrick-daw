## Stage C2 part 2b: play chords live in CHORD LAB, REC WHAT I PLAY

- While the loop plays, a click on a chord in CHORD LAB (the palette or a WHAT FITS card) sounds from the next beat (LAUNCH NOW: the next step) through the scene's PLAY layer, and holds until the next chord of the loop. Stopped, a click only lets you hear it, as before.
- REC WHAT I PLAY (THE LOOP row): every chord played that way is written into the loop where it sounded, one undo step each.
- `Sequencer::QueueLiveChord` / `LiveChordSerial` / `LiveChordStart` / `LiveChordLen`; the chord start of the sequencer is one function (`StartChord`) for the loop and the live chord.
