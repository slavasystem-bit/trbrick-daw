## Stage C2 part 2a: the PLAY layer of the chord track

- CHORD LAB has a PLAY column: STACK / ARP / STRUM / RHYTHM, RATE (SPREAD for STRUM), GATE, OCTAVES, ORDER and SMOOTH VOICES, with a picture of how the chord of the chosen place is played.
- PLAY belongs to the scene (`Pattern::chordPlay`) and the sequencer applies it while it plays (`core/ChordPerform.h`): the chord steps keep only what sounds, so a live chord goes through the same performer. The default (STACK, gate 100 %, 1 octave, voices as written) plays exactly as before.
- Saved per scene as `chordPlay=` only when changed; project format 11.
- Every PLAY change is one undo step of the scene (UNDO ALL in the lab takes it back too).
