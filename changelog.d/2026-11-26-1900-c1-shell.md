## Stage C1 (part 2): two tabs, three tools that open the same way

ROADMAP stage C1 "Shell and words", audit v44.58 items UI-1, UI-4 and UI-9; mockup `docs/mockups/c1_shell.png`, approved by the author on 2026-11-26. No change to sound or to project files.

- **UI-1: only LIVE and TRACK are tabs.** MIX LAB, CHORD LAB and MASTER FX are three tool buttons in the tab row with their key next to the name (M, C, F). Each opens in the same place, the room under the header of the screen you are on, under one strip: name, what it does, the key, x. One tool at a time (opening another replaces it); the same key, x or Esc closes it and you are back where you were. View lists LIVE / TRACK (Tab) and the three tools with their keys, checked while open. Tab also closes an open tool.
- MIX LAB is no longer a third tab: it opens over LIVE or TRACK and the tab stays. MASTER FX is no longer a popover (Esc did not close it). CHORD LAB opens in the same room; the sequencer above it is gone (approved), its own jam loop shows the chords. Its JAM / SOUND switch stays; the x of the panel moved to the strip.
- **UI-9: the header shows what fits the screen.** LAUNCH only on LIVE. The MASTER button is gone: the chain switch is CHAIN ON / OFF in MASTER FX, and the MASTER FX button shows a teal dot while the chain is on.
- **UI-4: PITCH has no hidden keyboard mode.** Option+Up / Down = faster / slower by 0.1 % (Shift 1 %), Option+Left / Right = a push while held, at any time. Return always plays from the brick; P and R do nothing any more. A click on the PITCH value still shows the fader and the range (Esc hides it); double click or Option+click = 0 %.
- The "?" screen lists Tab, M / C / F and Option+arrows.
- State: `AppState::Tool` (`tool`, `OpenTool`, `ToggleTool`, `CloseTool`, `ChordLabOpen`) replaces `chordLabOpen`, the MIX LAB tab and `masterFxRequest`; `HeaderPanel::DrawMasterFx` replaces `DrawMenus`.
- Selftest: source ratchets for the tabs, the tool buttons and keys, View, PITCH keys and the header; one-tool-at-a-time state checks.
