## Stage C4b: 1/2 BEAT fill, review polish

- Moving a half-beat fill on TRACK can not go past the start of the pass any more (the limit counts half a beat, not zero).
- The fill block on TRACK and on the FILL page is drawn as long as it sounds: half a beat on the triplet grid is one step (a third of a beat).
- A fill length of 0 is read as half a beat only from format 10 files; an older file keeps reading it as one beat, as before.
