## Stage C3 (review of #57): instrument cells take the arrows only under the mouse; Shift is finer

- In the instrument block, Up / Down change the cell under the mouse only while the mouse is on it (`ContextPanel::KeyCell`: last frame's hovered cell, else the selected one). Before, the hovered cell became the selected one, so the arrows kept changing it after the mouse had left. Click and Left / Right still choose the selected cell, which the arrows change when the mouse is elsewhere.
- Shift + Up / Down is finer on instrument cells too (one tenth of a step), like on every field and fader; it used to be 4-5 times bigger. A patch value (SOUND tab) moves one step either way, its smallest.
- The block's key hint and the cell hints say so; 2 updated `t8_selftest` source checks.
