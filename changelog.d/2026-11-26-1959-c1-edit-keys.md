## Stage C1 (part 3): the Mac delete key removes a brick, Cmd+C / Cmd+V copy notes in the piano roll

ROADMAP stage C1, audit v44.58 item UI-8; the keys table of the approved mockup `docs/mockups/c1_shell.png`. No change to sound or to project files.

- **Delete or Backspace removes the selected brick** (or the selected range) on the TRACK strip. Before, only Delete did, which is fn+delete on a MacBook; the piano roll already took both.
- **Cmd+C / Cmd+V in the piano roll copy and paste the selected notes** while the mouse is over the roll. A copied note keeps its whole step (length, velocity, probability, nudge ...) and its place relative to the first copied note. Cmd+V puts the first note at the column under the mouse (pulled back so the last one still fits), the pasted notes become the selection, one Undo step. On a chord track a pasted note joins the chord of that step. The copied notes stay for other tracks and scenes. Elsewhere Cmd+C / Cmd+V still copy the whole track, Shift+Cmd+C / V the whole scene.
- `CopyNotes` / `PasteNotes` in `core/NoteEdit.h` (tested); `AppState::rollCopyFrame` / `rollPasteFrame` tell the shortcuts when the keys belong to the roll.
