## Stage C2 (part 1): CHORD LAB shows what fits in a place of the loop

- New layout, as in the approved CHORD LAB v2 mockup: HARMONY (the palette) and THE LOOP on the left, WHAT FITS IN BAR N on the right. A JAM | SOUND switch still opens the chord instrument; PLAY and a short SOUND column come in the next parts of C2.
- Colour = function in the key (`core/ChordAdvice.h`): home dark (I, III, and VI in a major key), away middle (II, IV, VI in a minor key), tension bright (V, VII, any dominant 7th or diminished triad). A borrowed chord: on the tonic it is home, a dominant shape or one on the 5th or 7th degree is tension, any other is away. Borrowed chords are dashed. A white frame marks the chord sounding now. The bar under a name shows how many notes under the advised place the chord holds.
- A tension line over the loop: one level per block, home low, tension high.
- WHAT FITS is about the selected block, or the room after the last block (drawn dashed as "next chord ?"). It shows the four best chords in the chosen mode (BEST / SMOOTH / MORE TENSION / NEW COLOUR), each with three measured bars:
  - FUNCTION: how the chord's function suits the next chord (tension before home is the strongest pull);
  - VOICES: semitones the voices move, with each voice going to a different nearest tone;
  - MELODY: how many of the notes under the place the chord holds.

  One sentence below names the facts: the leading tone into the next root, how many voices move, stay or are added, which notes the chord holds, and the half-step rubs. Click = hear the chord, double-click = put it in the place, drag = drop it into the loop.
- Header: A BEFORE / B DRAFT plays the scene's own chords or the draft. Nothing is written, and an edit always goes back to the draft. Also in the header: KEEP IN SCENE (was ADD TO SCENE), UNDO ALL (takes back the lab's own undo steps since it was opened, under the scene lock), and THROW ONTO THE TRACK / BRICK n PLAYS THIS JAM.
- MATCH is gone: the notes are always read. The source tracks are now small BASS / SYN 2 / SYN 3 toggles. Block length, split and delete moved to the block's right-click menu. The WHY, SIZE and FROM JAM TO TRACK boxes are gone.
- BRICK n PLAYS THIS JAM now takes the scene lock (it pushed an undo step without it).
