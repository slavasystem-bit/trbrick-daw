1 The 11th instrument: SAMPLER (row under Chord in the sequencer, same M and S, same colour system)

Perc Chord Sampler

The sampler is a normal row: steps, M, S, mute masks, bricks, LIVE bars (11 bars now), WHO PLAYS, MIX LAB, stems. What a step plays depends on the MODE below: ONE-SHOT = the sample at the pitch of the step's note, SLICES = the step picks a slice (the note number is the slice number), LOOP = the step starts the loop (it runs to the end or until the next step).

SLICES: marks the hits of a break (transients) or cuts it in even parts; TO PATTERN writes the slices as steps in the order they come, so a chopped break plays back as it was, then you rearrange the steps. LOOP: you say how many beats the sample is (or let the BPM come from its length); it plays at project BPM / SAMPLE BPM times the speed, so the pitch moves with the tempo, as on a turntable. No time-stretch in this release, honestly: a big tempo change changes the sound.
