One STYLE row above KIT and BEAT (which stay as they are for picking one part by hand). APPLY puts the whole style onto the scene you are editing; the four chips say which parts it may touch: BEAT (drums, swing, groove, kit, scene length), SOUND (bass, synth 2, synth 3 and chord presets), NOTES (bass line and chords in the current KEY and SCALE; the key is never changed), TEMPO (BPM). TEMPO is off by default so a style never changes the tempo of a set by surprise. One undo step for everything. Nothing plays or changes until you press APPLY.

click = select  ·  Enter / double-click = apply with the chips of the panel (Undo brings it all back)  ·  Esc = close  ·  star = favourite

Cards are filterable like TYPICAL BEATS (category, tags). The strip under the cards says exactly what APPLY will do for the selected style, in the words of the panel; the parts you switched off by the chips are shown dim. Greyed cards are the styles that are planned for the next releases and not in this one. MY STYLES: later, a style made from the current scene (beat, kit, presets, chords) with SAVE.
