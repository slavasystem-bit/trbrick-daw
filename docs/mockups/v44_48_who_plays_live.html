Ten instrument tiles with the same icons and colours as WHO PLAYS and the sequencer: click = mute, S under = solo. They replace the four single pads KICK / BASS / SYN2 / SYN3 (and add the clap, open hat, perc and chord that had no pad). The groups stay: ALL DRUMS, DRUMS (snare + clap + perc), HATS, MUTE ALL, DROP. ALL ON (new, dashed green) = every mute and solo of the scene off in one press. Muted bars are dim, bars silenced by someone's solo are dim with a dashed outline. Everything reads the same switches as the M and S of the sequencer, so the three places never disagree.
